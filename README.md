## What We Do

### 💰 Budget Management
Tracking income and expenses, maintaining financial records, and providing regular reporting to the host so you always know where things stand.

### 🤝 Supplier Communications
Coordinating with vendors, accommodations, transportation providers, and activity operators — all on your behalf.

### 🌐 Registration Page
Creation and hosting of your retreat registration page, including room descriptions, pricing, availability, and all participant-facing materials.

### 📧 Participant Communications
Managing pre- and post-sale support, trip planning guidance, and all email correspondence with your participants.

### 📖 Travel Guide & Planning
Producing and distributing a curated guide covering logistics, local information, and travel preparation — plus limited travel booking support.

### 🗂️ Organization & Logistics
Managing timelines, logistics, and day-to-day coordination before, during, and after your retreat.

### 🏦 Banking & Accounting
Managing participant payments, issuing invoices, reconciling accounts, and maintaining complete financial records for your retreat.

### 🏡 Retreat Property Search
Don't have a retreat property in mind yet? **We can help you find the perfect venue** — connecting you with properties that match your group's size, style, and budget.
